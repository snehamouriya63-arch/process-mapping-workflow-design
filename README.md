# Process Mapping & Workflow Design

## Project Title

Improving Online Food Delivery Process

---

## 1. AS-IS Process (Current Process)

### Current Workflow

Start

↓

Customer places order through mobile application

↓

Restaurant receives order

↓

Restaurant confirms order

↓

Food preparation begins

↓

Operations team manually searches for an available delivery partner

↓

Is a delivery partner available?

No → Wait and search again

Yes → Assign delivery partner

↓

Delivery partner reaches restaurant

↓

Food picked up

↓

Food delivered to customer

↓

End

---

## 2. Problems Identified

### Problem 1: Manual Delivery Partner Assignment

The operations team manually searches for available delivery partners, which increases processing time.

### Problem 2: Delivery Delays

If no delivery partner is immediately available, the order remains pending, resulting in delayed deliveries.

### Problem 3: Limited Customer Visibility

Customers receive limited information about their order status during delivery.

### Problem 4: Increased Operational Effort

Staff spend significant time assigning delivery partners instead of focusing on other operational activities.

---

## 3. TO-BE Process (Improved Process)

### Proposed Workflow

Start

↓

Customer places order through mobile application

↓

Restaurant receives and confirms order

↓

Food preparation begins

↓

System automatically identifies the nearest available delivery partner

↓

Delivery partner assigned automatically

↓

Customer receives real-time order tracking notifications

↓

Delivery partner collects food from restaurant

↓

Food delivered to customer

↓

Customer provides feedback through the application

↓

End

---

## 4. Benefits of the Improved Process

1. Reduces delivery partner assignment time.
2. Minimizes order delivery delays.
3. Improves customer satisfaction through real-time tracking.
4. Reduces manual effort and operational costs.
5. Enhances overall process efficiency.
6. Improves customer retention and user experience.

---

## 5. One-Page Summary Report

The current online food delivery process relies on manual assignment of delivery partners after food preparation. This manual activity creates delays, especially during peak business hours when delivery demand is high. Customers often experience longer waiting times and limited visibility regarding the status of their orders. As a result, customer satisfaction decreases and operational costs increase.

The proposed TO-BE process introduces an automated delivery partner assignment system. The system automatically identifies and assigns the nearest available delivery partner, significantly reducing the time required for order processing. In addition, customers receive real-time tracking updates, providing transparency throughout the delivery journey.

By eliminating manual assignment activities and improving communication with customers, the new process reduces delivery delays, improves operational efficiency, and enhances customer satisfaction. This redesigned workflow supports faster deliveries, better resource utilization, and improved business performance.
